00 The four classes Reference

Given — the four configuration classes

MultirotorFour or more fixed rotors. Hovers; everything rides on hover power
Fixed-wingA wing, forward flight only. Cannot stop; needs a way up and down
HelicopterOne rotor, variable pitch. Best hover there is, complex and costly
VTOL hybridWing plus lift rotors. Wing range, vertical launch — and two systems to carry

01 Mission 1 · Film Core

A crew wants a six-minute continuous shot following a car along a coast road at 80 km/h, then climbing away into a wide static view of the bay.

02 Mission 2 · Pipeline Core

Every kilometre of a 400 km gas pipeline, photographed twice a year at 5 cm/pixel. Access roads run alongside for most of its length.

03 Mission 3 · Port Core

The underside of every crane boom in a container port checked for cracks, close up, without stopping container movements.

04 Mission 4 · Research Core

Three kilograms of samples moved 40 km from a remote valley station to a road head, twice a week, all year, in whatever weather turns up.

05 If you finish early Stretch

One of the four is genuinely arguable. Find it.

06 Discussion — two minutes Core

Pick one mission to talk about, and be ready with all four answers for it.
